Canadian deadlines

Key CRA dates

Personal tax (T1)April 30. Self-employed individuals have until June 15 to file, but any balance owing is still due April 30.
Corporate tax (T2)Six months after your fiscal year end. Balance owing is generally due two or three months after year end.
GST/HST returnsMonthly and quarterly filers: one month after the period ends. Annual filers: three months after year end for most.
Payroll remittancesUsually the 15th of the following month for regular remitters. Frequency depends on your average withholding.
T4 and T4A slipsLast day of February for the previous calendar year.

When a date falls on a weekend or holiday, CRA moves it to the next business day. Dates vary by situation, so confirm yours with us or on canada.ca.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

When do I need to register for GST/HST?

Most businesses must register once taxable sales pass $30,000 over four consecutive calendar quarters. You can register earlier to claim input tax credits. Ontario uses the 13% HST.

How long should I keep my records?

CRA generally requires you to keep tax records for six years from the end of the last tax year they relate to.

What documents should I bring for my personal return?

T4, T4A, T5 and T3 slips, RRSP receipts, tuition slips (T2202), medical and donation receipts, and rental or self-employment records. We send a checklist after your call.

Can you help if I received a CRA letter?

Yes. Send us a copy right away. We read the notice, tell you what it means, and prepare the response.

Do you work with clients outside Scarborough?

Yes. We work with clients across Ontario and Canada by secure upload, phone and video call.

Do you provide audits or reviews?

No. We prepare books, payroll, tax filings and management financial statements. We do not issue audit, review or other assurance reports.

How much do your services cost?

Fees depend on the work and the state of your records. You receive a written quote before we begin.
